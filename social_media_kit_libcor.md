# Social Media Announcement: GentsConcerts x LIBCOR Partnership

**Headline:** United by Heritage, Driven by Purpose 🇱🇷🇷🇼

We are thrilled to announce that GentsConcerts has officially partnered with the Liberian Community in Rwanda (LIBCOR) for the **2nd All Liberian Festival Celebration**! Join us on Sunday, August 23, 2026, at ULK Gisozi in Kigali for an unforgettable day filled with cultural performances, panel discussions, football and kickball games, and vibrant community fellowship. 

To ensure a seamless experience for our community, all tickets are now live and accessible exclusively through our digital platform. Attendees can secure their digital passes instantly without paper hassle, featuring a secure QR code for fast entry at the gate on festival day. 

Head over to [gentsconcerts.netlify.app](https://gentsconcerts.netlify.app/) right now to claim your ticket. Follow the simple step-by-step guide below to sign up, log in, and secure your spot at Liberia's premier celebration!

---

# Step-by-Step Navigational Guide: How to Get Your Ticket

### **Step 1: Access the Portal**
Visit **[gentsconcerts.netlify.app](https://gentsconcerts.netlify.app/)** on your smartphone, tablet, or desktop. The platform is fully responsive and optimized to give you a pristine, medium-zoomed experience across all devices.

### **Step 2: Sign Up or Log In**
Tap **"Sign Up"** on the platform. Choose **"Fan / Attendee"**, enter your name, email, and phone number, and create a secure password. If you already have an account, simply click **"Log In"** with your credentials.

### **Step 3: Locate the Festival**
On the home page, scroll down or check the featured banner to find the **All Liberian Festival 2026**. Tap the event card to view full details, including the official LIBCOR mission, venue schedule (ULK Gisozi), and countdown timer.

### **Step 4: Acquire Your Ticket**
Click **"Get Tickets"**, select your desired ticket tier (Regular Access), and confirm your claim. Your ticket is generated instantly with a unique digital QR code that lives securely in your profile dashboard.

### **Step 5: Event Day Entry**
On August 23, simply open your profile on your phone and present your digital QR code at the gate. Enjoy a fast, paperless entry and celebrate in style with the Liberian community in Rwanda!
