## **Recording Instructions for Voiceover:**
1.  **Pacing:** Keep a steady, moderate pace. Pause slightly between sections to allow for visual transitions.
2.  **Emphasis:** Emphasize key terms like "Instant," "Automated," "Secure," and "Real-time Data."
3.  **Tone:** Sound enthusiastic but professional, especially when mentioning the LIBCOR partnership.
